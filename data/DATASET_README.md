# NexaSphere Retail — BuildFest Synthetic Business Intelligence Dataset

Purpose:
A synthetic, internally consistent dataset designed specifically for the AI BuildFest 2026
AI Business Intelligence Assistant case study.

Business context:
NexaSphere Retail Ltd. is an omnichannel electronics and home-appliance retailer operating
physical stores, fulfilment operations, digital channels and corporate sales.

The dataset supports:
- Revenue and gross-profit analysis
- Margin analysis
- Product/category/brand comparisons
- Store and regional comparisons
- Customer-segment analysis
- Marketing campaign ROI
- Delivery partner performance
- Returns analysis
- Inventory stockout/excess-stock analysis
- Employee/store performance
- Target-vs-actual analysis
- Natural-language business questions
- Evidence-based management recommendations

Important:
GROUND_TRUTH_INTERNAL.json is an internal validation file for the development team.
Do not expose it in the public demo or repository if it would reveal planted findings.

The dataset contains intentionally planted business conditions so that the product can
demonstrate real decision-support value:
- Revenue growth with margin pressure caused partly by increased discounting/product mix
- Elevated returns in an audio product category
- Delivery deterioration for one partner
- Stockouts for selected high-demand TV products/stores
- Excess inventory for selected air-conditioner products/stores
- A high-performing marketing campaign
