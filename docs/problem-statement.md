# Problem Statement

## The business scenario (Case Study 4)

NexaSphere Retail Ltd. is an omnichannel electronics and home-appliance
retailer operating physical stores, fulfilment hubs, digital channels and
corporate sales. The company collects large volumes of sales, customer,
inventory, marketing, employee and delivery data. Regular reports exist, but
management still struggles to quickly understand what is actually driving
performance: revenue may look healthy while margin, delivery performance,
returns or customer satisfaction quietly deteriorate underneath it.

## The real problem

It isn't a data shortage. NexaSphere already has sales, product, customer,
inventory, delivery, marketing, employee and target data. The problem is
**interpretation speed**: a manager who wants to answer "why did profit not
grow with revenue?" has to manually open several spreadsheets, join them in
their head, and decide what's worth investigating — every single time a
question comes up.

Traditional BI dashboards don't solve this. They present numbers; they don't
explain them, connect them across datasets, or tell a manager what to do
next. A generic "chat with your CSV" AI tool doesn't solve it either — most
of those let a language model do the arithmetic, which means a manager can
no longer trust the numbers without checking them by hand, defeating the
purpose.

## Who is affected

Operations and commercial managers at a growing multi-channel retailer, who
need to make weekly or monthly decisions about pricing, inventory
allocation, delivery partner contracts, marketing spend and store
performance — without a dedicated data analyst on call for every question.

## What "solved" looks like

A manager should be able to open one screen and immediately see the 3-5
things that need their attention this week, each with the evidence behind
it and a concrete next step — and separately, be able to ask a plain-English
question and get an answer computed from the real data, not guessed by a
language model.
